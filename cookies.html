Cookie Policy

The cookies this website uses and why.

This website uses only the cookies it needs to work. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Cookies we use

  • ec_sess — keeps your bag, your sign-in and form security working as you move between pages. Deleted when you close your browser.
  • ec_cur — remembers the currency you chose to view prices in. Lasts 6 months.

Because these cookies are strictly necessary, or only store a preference you set, they don’t need consent under the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations.

Payment pages

When you pay, you move to Stripe or PayPal, which set their own cookies under their own policies.

Changes

If we ever add analytics or marketing cookies, we will ask for your consent first and update this page.
