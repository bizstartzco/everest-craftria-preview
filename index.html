How pashmina is made

Slow by nature, soft by design

Pashmina is the fine undercoat of Himalayan mountain goats. Turning it into cloth is still largely hand work — and every step shows in the finished piece.

  1. Combing

    The soft undercoat is combed out in spring, when the goats naturally moult it.

  2. Spinning

    The fibre is so fine that it is traditionally spun by hand into yarn.

  3. Weaving

    Yarn is woven on a loom, often in a blend with silk for strength and drape.

  4. Finishing

    Pieces are washed, dyed and fringed by hand before they are ready to wear.
