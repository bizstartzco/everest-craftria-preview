Privacy Notice

What personal data we collect, why, and your rights under UK data protection law.

This notice explains how Everest Craftria LTD (“we”) uses your personal data. We are the data controller. Contact us about privacy through our contact page or at [TO CONFIRM: privacy email address].

What we collect

  • When you order: your name, email, phone number, delivery address and order details.
  • When you create an account: your name, email and a securely hashed password (we can’t see your password).
  • When you contact us: your name, email and your message.
  • When you subscribe: your email address.
  • Payments: handled by Stripe or PayPal. We receive confirmation of payment but never your full card details.

Why we use it, and our lawful basis

  • To take, deliver and support your order — *performance of a contract*.
  • To keep accounting and tax records — *legal obligation*.
  • To run your account and wishlist — *performance of a contract*.
  • To reply to your messages — *legitimate interests*.
  • To send our newsletter — *consent*, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • To prevent fraud and keep the site secure — *legitimate interests*.

Who we share it with

Only the service providers we need to run the shop: our payment processors (Stripe, PayPal), delivery carriers, our web host (Hostinger) and our email provider. [TO CONFIRM: list any other providers, e.g. accountant or marketing tools.] We do not sell your data.

International transfers

Some providers may process data outside the UK. Where they do, we rely on UK adequacy regulations or appropriate safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum. [TO CONFIRM: if orders are fulfilled from Nepal, say so and describe the safeguard used.]

How long we keep it

Order and accounting records are kept for six years after the end of the financial year they relate to, as UK law requires. Account data is kept while your account is open. Newsletter data is kept until you unsubscribe.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to data portability. To use any of these rights, contact us. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).
