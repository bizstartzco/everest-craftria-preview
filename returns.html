Returns & Refunds

How to cancel an order or return a piece, and when you’ll be refunded.

Your right to cancel

If you buy from us online as a consumer in the UK, you have the right to cancel your order within 14 days of the day you receive it, without giving a reason. This comes from the Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013.

How to return a piece

  1. Contact us within 14 days of delivery with your order number, telling us you want to cancel.
  2. Send the piece back within 14 days of telling us. The return address is: [TO CONFIRM: returns address].
  3. Please return pieces unworn, with any labels and packaging, and use a tracked service.

Unless the item is faulty or not as described, return postage is paid by you. [TO CONFIRM: or state that returns are free, and update Settings → Prices, delivery & returns to match.]

Refunds

We refund the price of the item and the standard delivery charge you paid, to your original payment method, within 14 days of receiving the return (or proof you sent it back, if sooner). If you chose a more expensive delivery option, we refund only the cost of our cheapest standard option. We may reduce the refund if the item has been handled more than needed to inspect it.

Faulty or not as described

Under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 you are entitled to a repair, replacement or refund if goods are faulty, not as described or not fit for purpose. Please contact us with photos and we will cover the return cost.

Made-to-order and personalised pieces

[TO CONFIRM: If you offer pieces made or personalised to a customer’s specification, the 14-day cancellation right may not apply to them — take advice and describe the rule here. Standard made-to-order stock items are normally still returnable.]

Natural variation

Handmade pieces vary slightly in colour, size and texture. This is part of how they are made and is not a fault.
